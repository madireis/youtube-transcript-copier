## Detailed Description (Copy & Paste to CWS Console)

```text
Extract, search, bookmark, and export transcripts from any YouTube video or Short in seconds. Send full transcripts directly into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini with one click.

YouTube Transcript Copier is a fast, privacy-first Chrome extension built for researchers, students, content creators, and professionals who need accurate text from video content without manual scrubbing.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🌟 CORE FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚡ ONE-CLICK TRANSCRIPT EXTRACTION
• Seamlessly extracts complete transcripts from standard YouTube videos and YouTube Shorts.
• Automatically reveals hidden transcript panels without page reloads.
• Native "Copy Transcript" buttons integrated cleanly into YouTube's interface.

🔍 SEARCH EVERY WORD WITH TIMESTAMPS
• Real-time search inside the transcript text.
• Jump directly to the exact second in the video by clicking any timestamp.
• Instant match count and visual highlighting.

🔖 SAVE KEY MOMENTS & NOTES
• Bookmark important sentences and highlights during playback.
• Review saved moments in a dedicated quick-access side panel.
• Automatically assemble your bookmarks into a clean Markdown study guide.

🤖 DIRECT 1-CLICK AI INTEGRATION
• Send formatted transcripts directly into ChatGPT, Claude, or Google Gemini.
• Ready-made prompt templates: Executive Summary, Key Insights, Action Items, and Q&A.
• Auto-opens your preferred AI assistant with your transcript ready to analyze.

📁 MULTIPLE EXPORT FORMATS
• Plain Text (.txt) — clean text with or without timestamps.
• Subtitles (.srt & .vtt) — ready for video editing software like Premiere, DaVinci, or CapCut.
• Markdown (.md) — structured headings and clickable timestamps for Obsidian, Notion, and Bear.
• Developer Formats (.json & .csv) — raw data for analytics, translation, and NLP workflows.

🌐 MULTILINGUAL TRANSCRIPT SUPPORT
• Switch between all available video languages with an instant dropdown.
• Supports auto-generated captions and human-transcribed subtitle tracks.

📚 PLAYLIST & BATCH EXTRACTION
• Scan full YouTube playlists or channel video tabs.
• Select multiple videos with visual checkboxes and extract them in parallel.
• Download all transcripts at once in a single bundled ZIP archive.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🚀 HOW TO USE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Open any YouTube video or YouTube Short.
2. Click the "Copy Transcript" button near the video actions, or click the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar (or press Ctrl+Shift+Y / Cmd+Shift+Y).
3. Search, bookmark, or switch formats (TXT, SRT, VTT, Markdown, JSON, CSV).
4. Click Copy or Export — or click Send to AI to summarize instantly.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY FIRST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Your privacy is our priority. YouTube Transcript Copier operates entirely inside your browser:
• No tracking, telemetry, or analytics.
• No account registration or external server processing.
• Transcripts and bookmarks are stored strictly on your local machine using Chrome's secure local storage API.
• Host permissions are restricted solely to YouTube for transcript extraction and your chosen AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) when using the AI export feature.

• macOS: Command + Shift + Y
(Shortcuts can be customized anytime via chrome://extensions/shortcuts)
```
