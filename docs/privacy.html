Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

YouTube Transcript Copier is designed from the ground up with a strict privacy-first philosophy. We believe your data belongs to you. The extension functions entirely within your browser client.

2. Data Collection & Analytics

We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII). No browsing history, account credentials, or video view history is ever logged or transferred to any remote servers.

3. How YouTube Content Is Processed

When you request a transcript on a YouTube video or Shorts page, the extension extracts the subtitles directly from YouTube's existing player interface in your active tab. All formatting (timestamps, markdown, clean text) happens locally on your computer.

4. AI Provider Interactions

If you choose to use the 1-Click Send to AI features (ChatGPT, Claude, or Google Gemini), the transcript is forwarded directly from your browser to the designated AI web application tab. We do not intermediate this transmission.

5. Local Storage

Your preferences (such as default export format, timestamp display, or theme mode) are stored locally in Chrome's chrome.storage.local or sync and never leave your browser.

6. Contact & Source Code

YouTube Transcript Copier is open source. You can inspect the complete source code on GitHub.
