Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

How do I download or copy a transcript from a YouTube video?

Simply install the YouTube Transcript Copier extension. When viewing any video on YouTube, click the "Copy Transcript" button located next to the video controls or use shortcut Ctrl+Shift+Y. The complete transcript is saved straight to your clipboard in seconds.

Can I copy YouTube transcripts without timestamps?

Yes! In the extension settings or popup menu, you can toggle timestamps on or off. You can export plain flowing text (paragraph mode) or choose formatted Markdown with clickable timestamp links.

Does this extension work with YouTube Shorts?

Yes, YouTube Shorts are fully supported. The extension automatically extracts captions from vertical Shorts reels just like regular long-form videos.

How does the 1-Click Send to ChatGPT/Claude work?

When you click the ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini button in the extension popup, it opens the AI web interface in a new tab and automatically pastes the full transcript along with your chosen prompt template (e.g., "Summarize this video").

Is YouTube Transcript Copier free?

Yes, it is 100% free and open source under the MIT License. There are no subscriptions, paywalls, or feature limits.

Is my data safe and private?

Completely. Everything executes locally in your browser. We do not track your browsing history, collect telemetry, or store user information. You can review our full Privacy Policy or audit the code on GitHub.
